Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Offline-First Architecture

ARCANA Forensics operates strictly offline on your analysis workstation. No case data, disk images, or triage artifacts are transmitted to external servers.

2. Information Collection

We only collect billing details (email and payment identifiers) strictly necessary to process commercial transactions via our payment gateway (Stripe).

3. Third-Party Processors

Payments are securely handled by Stripe. We do not store or process raw credit card numbers on our servers.
